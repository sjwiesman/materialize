## What this page will cover

A short decision page, not a feature matrix. Cloud for teams who want the
service operated for them, Self-Managed for teams who need Materialize inside
their own infrastructure and compliance boundary, and the Emulator for local
development and evaluation.

For each: what it is good for, what it does not do, and the specific
capabilities that differ in ways that affect the rest of the documentation. The
Emulator, for example, does not support OAuth, which changes how you connect an
agent.

States which deployment the documentation assumes by default, so every later
page can be read without ambiguity.
